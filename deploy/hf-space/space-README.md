# Khmer Dub

Upload a video in any spoken language and get Khmer subtitles (.srt) and the video with a Khmer voice.
Everything runs with free, open-source tools: faster-whisper, NLLB-200, edge-tts (or MMS) and FFmpeg.

**This is a demo.** The free Space keeps nothing: accounts, uploads and results are deleted whenever it
restarts or goes to sleep. Download your files as soon as they are ready.

- Open the site at its own address (`https://<name>-<space>.hf.space`) so logins work.
- Password reset emails are not sent.
- The translation and the voice are machine-made and need a Khmer speaker to check them.
- Dubbed videos use an AI-generated voice; label them that way. Only upload videos you own or may translate.
